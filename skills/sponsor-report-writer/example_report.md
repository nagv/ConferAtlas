# Northwind Analytics: sponsorship report

*Covers Last calendar year (1 Jan 2025 - 31 Dec 2025).*

## Executive summary

Across 2 events you received 412 leads against a target of 380, or 108% of target, at an average cost of $155 per lead. Harbor City Data Summit 2025 was the standout, while Lakeside AI Forum 2025 finished just short of its target at 90%.

## Audience reached

Your sponsored events drew 2,690 registrations and 2,081 attendees, an 80% show-up rate.

- **Seniority:** Senior (31%) and Mid-level (27%) professionals made up most of the audience.
- **Roles:** Data Scientists (22%) and Software Engineers (19%) were the largest groups.
- **Industries:** Software (38%) led, followed by Financial Services (17%).
- **Countries:** most attendees came from the United States (71%), with Canada next (9%).

## Engagement

Attendees rated the 61 sessions 4.02 out of 5 on average, with an NPS of +8. They spent 34.6 minutes per session on average, answered 0.71 polls and asked 0.22 questions per session. Booth traffic across both events totalled 2,310 visits; this counts visits to all booths, not only yours.

## Leads vs. target

- **Harbor City Data Summit 2025** (Premier Booth): 268 leads against a target of 220, 122% of target, at $119 per lead.
- **Lakeside AI Forum 2025** (Premier Booth): 144 leads against a target of 160, 90% of target, at $222 per lead.
- **Total:** 412 leads against 380, 108% of target. You met your target at 1 of 2 events, and no event fell below 80%.

Booth conversations produced 251 leads, demos 118 and virtual channels 43.

## Highlights

- Harbor City Data Summit 2025 delivered 268 leads, beating its target by the widest margin at the lowest cost per lead ($119).
- Booth conversations were your strongest channel, with 251 of your 412 leads.
- The audience matched your goals well: Software was the largest attendee industry at 38%.

## Recommendations

- Prioritise in-person events like Harbor City Data Summit 2025, where your cost per lead was lowest.
- For hybrid events such as Lakeside AI Forum 2025, add a live demo slot to strengthen the online channel, which produced the fewest leads.
- Keep the Premier Booth package and staff it for peak session breaks, since booth conversations drive most of your leads.
