# Toronto Clinical Innovation Congress 2024: session abstracts

April 28 to April 29, 2024, Toronto. The ten best-attended sessions.

## Patient Care: lessons from the field

- **Track:** Patient Care
- **Speaker:** Diego Garcia
- **Format:** virtual
